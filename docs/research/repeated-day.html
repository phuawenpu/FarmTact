The same harvest, another day

A tiny playable design study inspired by repetition and difference in Hong Sang-soo's films. Look at the same crop twice. Notice which detail changes what it can do.

Offline storyboard, not a released game feature or a full planner run. It uses one booked order and one batch from FarmTact's synthetic fixture, with a hypothetical three-day delay. No data is sent or saved.

Bed A1 · Caixin · 44 kg

The crop is scheduled to be ready on 14 September.

The customer needs 22 kg on 14 September.

Planning date: 8 September 2026. For this small timing exercise, consider this batch alone and assume its yield is usable as scheduled, with no competing allocation.

Could planting today rescue this delivery?

In the declared demo recipe, caixin needs 7 nursery days plus 21 growing days. Sowing on 8 September puts maturity on 6 October. That cannot rescue a 14 September deadline. The opening 5 kg inventory also expires on 10 September.

This establishes a timing limit in the fixture. It does not calculate whole-farm fulfilment, competing orders, waste or margin. The complete planner must calculate those separately.

Try another look: switch passes, predict again, and check whether the unchanged 44 kg leads you to overlook the changed date.

The design question: did the second encounter change what you noticed? The harvest stayed the same size. Its usefulness to this order changed with time.
