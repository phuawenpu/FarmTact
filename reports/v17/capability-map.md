Rubric evidence remains separated: the seven criteria on organizer PDF page 13 are goals/scope, reasoning loop, tool integration, human checkpoints, security/guardrails, observability/evaluation and platform/tooling. See `personas/review.md` for observations and hypotheses, `personas/v17-acceptance.json` for actual frontend agent-loop transport behavior, and final regression for server-side contracts. Labelled gateway fixtures are not actual provider-quality trials. Neither scripted personas nor passing workflow tests establish real-user comprehension.

The final completion audit must inspect final test outcomes, the exact release artifact and preservation checks. This map records implemented paths and intended evidence; it does not waive a failed or missing release gate.
