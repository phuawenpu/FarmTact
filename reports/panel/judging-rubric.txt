Judging Rubric
1. Goal & Scope Definition 
• e.g., business value, clear purpose
2. Architecture & Reasoning Loop 
• e.g., appropriate planning pattern, explicit state/memory management
3. Tool Use & Integration 
• e.g., purpose-fit tools, well-typed schemas
4. Autonomy & Human-in-the-Loop 
• e.g., risk-calibrated autonomy, escalation checkpoints, etc.
5. Safety, Security & Guardrails 
• e.g., prompt-injection resistance, least-privilege access
6. Observability & Evaluation 
• e.g., logging/tracing of decisions, golden-path + adversarial eval cases, etc.
7. Platform & Tooling Usage 
• e.g., idiomatic framework use, clean multi-agent orchestration, etc.
