# V6 crop evidence implementation report

Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

FarmTact now has twelve crop knowledge profiles. The two additions are `garlic_chives` (*Allium tuberosum* Rottler ex Spreng.) and `sawtooth_coriander` (*Eryngium foetidum* L.). All ten previous profiles and the four-crop synthetic farm remain intact.

The evidence register adds four primary studies (`P21`–`P24`) with DOI, reviewed scope, specific finding, design implication, transfer limit and a parameter-use block. The source register adds the two official NParks plant records, the official SFA fresh-produce product list, and references to all four papers. The HS mapping recognizes SFA's specific sawtooth-coriander product code while retaining `07099990` as an unresolved broad trade series; garlic chives remain unresolved within the broad alliaceous category.

No paper value was converted into yield, duration, nutrient, salinity, spacing or shade defaults. No source image or full-text article content was copied. Detailed citations, common-name boundaries, evidence findings, image-reuse status and remaining gaps are recorded in `docs/research/v6_crop_evidence.md`.
